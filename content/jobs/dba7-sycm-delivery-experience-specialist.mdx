## Looking for a job where you stay active, work independently, and make great money?

We're hiring Delivery Associates to join our fast-growing team. If you like being on the move, working on your own, and making an impact every day — this job is for you.

## The role

Full-time Delivery Associate based out of our Hagerstown-area station. You'll drive an Amazon delivery van on daily routes through Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania, getting packages to households and businesses across the region.

## Pay and benefits

- **$20.50 / hour and up** to start
- **Paid time off** accruing from your first day
- **Health, dental, and vision insurance**
- **401(k) with 4% employer match**
- **Tuition assistance** up to $5,250 per year
- **Supplemental insurance**: critical illness, AD&D, and voluntary life
- **Real advancement** — every leader at Sycamore started as a driver, and we promote from within

## About Sycamore

Sycamore Logistics is a six-year-old Amazon Delivery Service Partner. We operate out of two stations in the Hagerstown, MD area with over 100 delivery associates on the road every day. Safety first. Technology enhanced. Community organization.
