## Our story

Sycamore Logistics was born during the pandemic, when getting essential goods to people's doors went from convenience to necessity overnight. We started small — a few drivers, a handful of routes, a clear sense that the work mattered.

Six years later, we're still small by design. We're a locally-rooted, veteran-owned business operating out of two stations in the Hagerstown, MD area. Our team of 100+ delivery associates is on the road every day, delivering Amazon packages to households and businesses across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania.

What's changed since the early days isn't the mission. It's the scale and the system. The mission is still the same: be the safest delivery company on the planet, take care of our team, and show up for our community.

## What we believe

We're built on three principles. They're not slogans — they're how we decide things, day in and day out.

### Safety first

Our number-one goal is to be the safest delivery company on the planet. Every choice we make — vehicles, training, routes, technology, coaching — is filtered through that lens first.

### Technology enhanced

We invest in the tools that help our team do the work better and safer. Not flash for the sake of flash — practical technology that makes a driver's day smoother and the road safer for everyone on it.

### Community organization

We're a driver-first company. Every leader at Sycamore started as a driver, and we promote from within. We donate time and goods to local schools and community organizations across the Hagerstown area because the communities we deliver in are the communities we're part of.

## Where we operate

Our two stations are in the Hagerstown, MD area. From there, our drivers cover routes across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania. If you're in the region and you've received an Amazon package over the last six years, there's a real chance one of our drivers handed it to you.
