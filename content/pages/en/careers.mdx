# Careers at Sycamore

We're growing, and we're hiring. Sycamore Logistics is a six-year-old Amazon Delivery Service Partner, operating out of two stations in the Hagerstown, MD area. Our drivers deliver across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania.

We're a driver-first organization. Every leader at Sycamore started as a driver. We support our team with real benefits, a real career path, and a real community.

## Why drivers join Sycamore

### Real benefits, starting day one

Most jobs make you wait 30, 60, even 90 days before health insurance kicks in. Not here. Your health, dental, and vision coverage starts your first day on the job. So do your 401(k) eligibility (with our 4% employer match), paid time off accrual, and supplemental coverage options including critical illness, AD&D, and voluntary life. We don't ask you to prove yourself before we take care of you.

### Tuition assistance up to $5,250 a year

Working toward a degree, a trade certification, or a new career skill? We'll help. Sycamore offers up to $5,250 per year in tuition assistance for accredited programs. It's on us — not a loan to pay back.

### A real career path, not a promise

Every single leader at Sycamore started as a driver. Every one. We don't bring outsiders in to oversee our team — we promote from within because the people who've done the route know how to run the route. If you want more responsibility, earn it, and stick with us, you'll move up. That's not a hope. It's how this company is built.

### A management team that shows up

Our operations and HR teams aren't faraway voices on a phone. They're at the station, they know your name, and they respond when something happens on your shift. Many of them came up as drivers themselves, so they know exactly what your day looks like — and they make decisions with that in mind.

### A team and a community

Sycamore is a driver-first organization. Our team looks out for each other on the road and off — covering shifts when someone needs help, getting new hires comfortable, sharing what works. As a company, we also show up beyond our team. We donate time and goods to local schools and community organizations across the Hagerstown area because "community organization" isn't just a tagline — it's how we want to operate, every day.

### Small, local, veteran-owned

We're not a giant national fleet, and we don't want to be. We're a six-year-old, locally-rooted, veteran-owned small business that knows our drivers' names and our community's needs. That matters — to us, and to the kind of people we hire.
