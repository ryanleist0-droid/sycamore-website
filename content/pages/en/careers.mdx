# Careers at Sycamore

We're growing, and we're hiring. Sycamore Logistics is a six-year-old Amazon Delivery Service Partner, operating out of two stations in the Hagerstown, MD area. Our drivers deliver across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania.

We're a driver-first organization. Every leader at Sycamore started as a driver. We support our team with real benefits, a real career path, and a real community.

## Why drivers join Sycamore

- **Real benefits from day one.** Health, dental, vision, 401(k) with 4% employer match, PTO accruing from day one.
- **Tuition assistance** up to $5,250 per year — go back to school on us.
- **Advancement opportunities.** Every leader at Sycamore started as a driver. We always promote from within.
- **A professional management team** that's got your back, every shift.
- **An internal community** of drivers who look out for each other, with a company that gives back to the communities we serve.
