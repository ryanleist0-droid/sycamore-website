## Who we are

Sycamore Logistics is a small, local, veteran-owned business based in the Hagerstown, MD area. We've been on the road for six years, delivering packages across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania as an Amazon Delivery Service Partner. Our team of 100+ delivery associates is the heart of this company — and every part of our operation is built around them.

We started during the pandemic, when getting essential goods to people's doors wasn't a luxury but a lifeline. We've grown a lot since then, but the mission hasn't changed: be the safest delivery company on the planet, take care of our team, and show up for our community.

## Why we're different

### Safety first

<InlineImage src="/images/sycamore_operations_013.jpg" alt="Sycamore driver loading the van for the day's route" />

Our number-one goal is to be the safest delivery company on the planet. That shapes every decision we make — the vehicles we run, the training we put new drivers through, the way we coach the team. Safety isn't a checkbox here. It's the foundation.

### Technology enhanced

<InlineImage src="/images/sycamore_operations_004.jpg" alt="Driver using in-cab technology during a Sycamore route" />

We invest in the technology that helps our drivers do their jobs better and safer. Better tools, smarter routes, real-time coaching — small things that add up to fewer surprises on the road and better outcomes at the end of every shift.

### Community organization

<InlineImage src="/images/sycamore_backpack-drive_007.jpg" alt="Sycamore team unloading backpacks for a school donation drive" />

We're a driver-first company. Every leader at Sycamore started as a driver, and we always promote from within. Beyond our team, we donate time and goods to local schools and community organizations across the region. We deliver packages for a living — but we also deliver for the community we live and work in.
