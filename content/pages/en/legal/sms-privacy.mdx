*Last updated: October 19, 2026*

This policy explains how Sycamore Logistics ("Sycamore", "we", "us") handles information related to the text
messages (SMS/MMS) we send and receive. It applies to:
- **Operational messages** to company-issued mobile devices used by our delivery drivers: dispatch communications,
  safety alerts, and route and schedule updates.
- **Recruiting messages** to job applicants who have agreed to receive texts about their application: application
  status, interview and orientation scheduling.

## Information we collect
- **Phone numbers:** the mobile number that sends us a message or that we message.
- **Message content:** the text, and any photos you send us, along with the time and delivery status of each
  message.
- **Related records:** for operational messages, the route, vehicle and work location associated with the company
  device on the day of the message. For recruiting messages, your application record.

## How we use it
- To communicate with our drivers and applicants about work, safety, routes, schedules and applications.
- To keep records of operational communications for safety, quality and compliance purposes.
- To honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.

## How we share it
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

**We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for
marketing or promotional purposes.** Mobile information and opt-in data will not be shared with any third parties.

We share information only with service providers that help us deliver the messages, such as our messaging carrier
and the software platform we use to run operations, and only to provide this service. We may also disclose it where
required by law.

## Retention and security
- **Message records** are kept as business records.
- **Photos and call recordings** are kept for 90 days unless they are needed for a specific matter, such as a safety
  or insurance review.
- **Security:** information is stored with access controls and encryption.

## Your choices
- **Opt out:** reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving texts from that number.
- **Help:** reply **HELP** for help.
- **Questions:** contact us at info@sycamorelogistics.org or (301) 673-9986.
- **Company-issued devices:** operational messages to these devices are governed by the Sycamore Logistics Employee
  Handbook (Mobile Device and Company Equipment Policy).

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply (on company-issued devices, these charges are paid by
Sycamore).
