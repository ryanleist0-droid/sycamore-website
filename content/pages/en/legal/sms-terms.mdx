## SMS Terms

These SMS Terms apply to text messages from Sycamore Logistics. Message and data rates may apply.

## 1. Programs
Sycamore Logistics ("Sycamore") operates two text messaging programs:
- **Sycamore Logistics Dispatch:** operational messages to company-issued mobile devices used by Sycamore delivery
  drivers. They include dispatch communications, safety alerts, and route and schedule updates, and replies to
  messages drivers send to our dispatch numbers.
- **Sycamore Logistics Recruiting:** messages to job applicants who have agreed to receive texts about their
  application, including status updates and interview or orientation scheduling links.

## 2. Consent
- **Dispatch:** employees consent by completing and e-signing a Text Messaging Consent form in our HR system
  (Paycom) during onboarding. Each company-issued
  phone also receives a reminder of these terms, with STOP and HELP instructions, when it is assigned to a driver for
  the day. Sycamore starts texts only to company-issued phones; it replies, within 24 hours, to anyone who texts a
  dispatch number first.
- **Recruiting:** applicants opt in when they apply, by agreeing to receive text messages.
- **Consent is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.**

## 3. Message frequency
Message frequency varies with operations and with the status of an application.

## 4. Costs
Message and data rates may apply. On company-issued devices, these charges are paid by Sycamore.

## 5. Opting out and help
- **Stop:** reply **STOP** to any message, and you will receive one confirmation and no further texts from that program's
  numbers to that phone (for Dispatch, all of Sycamore's dispatch numbers).
- **Resume:** reply **START** to resume.
- **Help:** reply **HELP** for help, or contact info@sycamorelogistics.org / (301) 673-9986.
- **Opting out has no disciplinary consequence.** If a company-issued phone is opted out, dispatch reaches the driver
  by other means until texts are resumed.

## 6. Carriers
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
