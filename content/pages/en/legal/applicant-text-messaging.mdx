*Published: November 13, 2026*

Sycamore Logistics LLC ("Sycamore Logistics") sends text messages to job applicants about their own application
only when they opt in. Texting is **optional and is not a condition of applying**: applicants who do not opt in are
contacted by email only.

## Where applicants opt in

Applicants apply online at [https://app.alliumproject.com/apply/DBA7](https://app.alliumproject.com/apply/DBA7),
which is linked from every job posting on our [Careers page](https://www.sycamore-logistics.com/en/careers). On the
contact step, after entering their mobile number and email, the applicant sees an **optional, unchecked** box beside
this statement:

> I agree to receive text messages from Sycamore Logistics about my application, interviews and orientation.
> Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Directly below the box are links to our:

- **SMS Privacy Policy:** [https://www.sycamore-logistics.com/en/legal/sms-privacy](https://www.sycamore-logistics.com/en/legal/sms-privacy)
- **SMS Terms & Conditions:** [https://www.sycamore-logistics.com/en/legal/sms-terms](https://www.sycamore-logistics.com/en/legal/sms-terms)

The exact statement shown and the time of consent are stored with each application. A Spanish version is shown to
applicants who choose Spanish.

## What applicants receive

Messages about their own application: a link to a short video interview, a link to choose an in-person interview
time, booking confirmations, reminders before appointments they booked, orientation details, and answers from our
hiring team when they reply with a question. No marketing. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time, or **HELP** for
help.
