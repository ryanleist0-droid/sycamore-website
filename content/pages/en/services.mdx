## Amazon Delivery Service Partner

Sycamore Logistics is an Amazon Delivery Service Partner — a small, locally-owned business contracted with Amazon to handle the last leg of the package's journey. The van shows up at your door, the package is in your hands a few seconds later, and the driver is part of our team.

We run two stations in the Hagerstown, MD area. From there, our 100+ delivery associates run routes every day across Maryland, West Virginia, Virginia, and Pennsylvania.

## How we work

### Safety first

<InlineImage src="/images/sycamore_operations_013.jpg" alt="Sycamore driver loading the van for the day's route" />

Every shift starts with a safety check. Every route is planned with safety in mind. Every driver gets ongoing coaching on the things that prevent accidents and protect both the team and the people we share the road with. We're working to be the safest delivery company on the planet, and we measure ourselves against that goal every day.

### Technology that helps

<InlineImage src="/images/sycamore_operations_014.jpg" alt="Driver using Sycamore's in-cab tools" />

We invest in the technology that helps our drivers stay safe, run efficient routes, and get the job done right. Better tools mean fewer surprises and better outcomes — for our team and for the customers who rely on us.

### Real local accountability

We're not a giant national fleet operated from somewhere far away. We're locally owned, locally operated, and locally accountable. When something goes right on your route, our drivers hear about it. When something goes wrong, we know within minutes.
