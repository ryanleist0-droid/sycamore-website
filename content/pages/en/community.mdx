## What it means to be a community organization

"Community organization" is one of three principles we built Sycamore around — and we hold ourselves to it the same way we hold ourselves to safety and technology. That means investing time and goods into the schools, food banks, and local organizations that make the Hagerstown area what it is. Not as a marketing pose. A regular part of how we operate.

Here's what that looks like.

## School backpack drives

<PhotoGallery name="backpack-drive" />

Each school year, our team gathers and delivers backpacks loaded with supplies to local schools. These photos are from our drop at Burke Street School. The schools tell us what their students need; our drivers and ops team make sure it gets there.

## Holiday donation drives

<PhotoGallery name="christmas-drive" />

In December our station turns into a donation drop-off point. Toys, coats, food — whatever our community needs that we can help move. The team takes shifts staffing the drop-off, and we route the donations to local partners who distribute to families.

## Thanksgiving meals

<PhotoGallery name="thanksgiving-meal" />

We organize Thanksgiving meals for our drivers and community members. Tables in the station parking lot, food we share, everyone welcome.

## Why it matters to us

Our drivers are part of the community before they're part of Sycamore. The schools we drop backpacks at are where their kids go. The neighborhoods we deliver to are where they live. The people we share Thanksgiving with are their neighbors. Showing up for the community isn't a side project — it's an extension of the work we do every day on the route.

If you're a local school, food bank, or community organization with a need we can help with, [get in touch](/contact). We don't promise we can do everything, but we listen and we show up when we can.
